Support

Questions, bug reports, or feedback about MakeItOurs — we'd like to hear from you. MakeItOurs is in staging testing and is not a public customer release.

Email

support@bengesoftwarellc.com

How to report a problem

Pick the path that fits what you want to tell us:

  • In the app (managed subscription): choose Report a Problem from a project's menu, next to a request's Request ID, or in Account Management. Pick what kind of report it is and what you were doing, then describe it. The app shows everything it sends: your account and request identifiers, the app version and your device, so we can trace the problem without asking you for them. Your report becomes a private ticket.
  • By email: anything about your account, billing, security, or your own designs, and any report from Bring Your Own Infrastructure, goes to support@bengesoftwarellc.com. Report security problems this way, never in public.
  • In public: share-safe bugs, ideas and questions can go in public GitHub issues. The form asks for the same details as the app. Everything there is public, so leave out personal and account details.

What happens next

Every report gets a reference such as MIO-4K7Q2D and lands on our private board with its type, area, platform and app version. The area you pick sends it to the part of MakeItOurs that owns it. Brandon Benge triages every report. If it repeats an existing report, we link them and keep one ticket open.

After an in-app report you get a status link that shows Received, In progress or Done. You can also add an email to hear when it's done; we keep that address only until the report closes. Public issues update on GitHub.

While MakeItOurs is in staging testing, we aim to acknowledge reports within one business day and triage them within two. Security reports are handled the same day.

Good to know

MakeItOurs uses AI to propose design changes and can, at your direction, push those changes to your repository or permanently delete a project. A few things worth keeping in mind:

  • AI-generated changes are proposals, not guarantees. After a build, choose Rebuild, Save, or Discard Changes — Save creates a version you can always return to from Activity, where every saved version has its own Revert.
  • Destructive actions, like deleting a project repository, always require your explicit confirmation and cannot be undone by us afterward.
  • Managed subscription: MakeItOurs manages the AI, project datastore, and complete build pipeline outside your direct control. You do not need an AI provider key and cannot select the AI provider. If a managed build fails, stalls, or reports an error, email support@bengesoftwarellc.com with the project and request details so we can investigate it.
  • Bring your own infrastructure: GitHub is the source of truth for your project. You choose and pay for a supported AI provider, supply its key, and manage your GitHub repository, Actions workflows, usage, and spending. GitHub Actions adds queue and workflow latency, and troubleshooting that infrastructure remains your responsibility. Local data on your Mac is not backed up by MakeItOurs.
  • You can edit a project in another IDE, but a later upgrade could overwrite files it manages. The only files guaranteed never to be touched by an upgrade are config.py, model.py, and drawing_annotations.py — changes anywhere else are outside what we can guarantee.
