MakeItOurs is being built for homeowners, DIYers, small builders and designers who want to turn a building idea into a model they can inspect, revise and own. The staging build produces checked geometry, drawings and export files from versioned project source. It is still under test and has not been released to customers.

This staging demonstration follows one request across iPhone and Mac: add a swing set on the east side of the yard, 15 feet south of the pool, then inspect and export the result. A slab and footing are the proposed first jobs for outside-user testing.

The first draft is allowed to look a little funny. Generative CAD is a conversation, not a magic trick. Complex objects commonly need one to three focused follow-up requests; MakeItOurs keeps every change inspectable, testable, and reversible while you refine it.

What you get

The capabilities being tested in staging. Customer availability and supported workflows will be confirmed in release notes.

💬 Plain language in

Describe the change you want. MakeItOurs proposes real Python source changes, validates and builds them, and shows you a preview before anything is accepted.

📐 Exact, inspectable models

Staging builds open in an interactive 3D viewer for inspection and measurement. Verify dimensions and suitability for your own use.

📤 Export to what you already use

Staging builds can produce drawings, STEP, IFC4, GLB and data files for use in other tools. These are not permit-ready drawing sets or purchase-unit materials lists.

🤝 Yours to keep and share

Staging projects keep source and history in Git, including an option to use your own GitHub repository. Hosted private sharing and collaboration are planned, not released features.

Hosted or on your infrastructure · staging options

The staging build has a hosted path and a bring-your-own-infrastructure path using your own GitHub and supported AI provider. Self-serve purchase, production pricing and public onboarding are still planned. See Bring your own infrastructure for that option, Privacy for what each path stores, or Security for how credentials are transported and stored.

Mac and iPhone · in testing

Native Mac and iPhone flows are being tested by the owner on staging. App Store distribution and broader device support have not been verified for a customer release.
