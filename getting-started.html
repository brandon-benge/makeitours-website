Getting Started

Make it yours… Make it ours. Turn a building idea into a model you can inspect, revise and take with you. There are two ways to work: Hosted, which needs the least setup, and Bring Your Own Infra, for people who want to run their own GitHub and AI provider.

Not open to the public yet.

MakeItOurs is in pre-launch testing on staging with the owner and invited testers. There is no public sign-up, purchase or App Store release yet, and no launch date. The steps on this page describe how it will work once it opens. Follow the roadmap and release notes for updates, and see what you can do today.

Hosted: the least setup

Hosted is for people who want to start building without setting up developer accounts. MakeItOurs runs the AI, the builds and the project storage for you, so you don't need a GitHub account or an AI provider key.

What Hosted takes care of

  • The AI connection, with no provider keys to manage.
  • Builds, checks and project storage on MakeItOurs servers.
  • Versions of every change, so you can go back.
  • Sharing with a viewer or an editor. Projects stay private until you share them.
  • Alerts to the MakeItOurs team when a run fails.

What you still provide

  • The MakeItOurs app on a Mac or iPhone.
  • An Apple ID, to sign in with Apple.
  • MPUs from a Pro subscription or a pack, bought in the app. See plans and pricing.
  • Your idea, and your own review of the result before you build.

See everything Hosted includes, what Privacy says Hosted stores, and how Security protects your sign-in.

Hosted or Bring Your Own Infra

Both paths build the same kind of checked model, drawings and files from versioned project source. The difference is who sets up and runs the accounts behind it.

Hosted also adds features such as Sawyer, the AI guide, focused drawings and photo-real views. Each one is explained on the Hosted page. The Mac app can also keep a project only on that Mac and publish it to GitHub later; that path is still in testing.

Your first Hosted project

These are the customer steps for when Hosted opens. They can't be followed yet.

  1. 1
    Get the app. Install MakeItOurs on your Mac or iPhone.
  2. 2
    Sign in with Apple. Open Account Management in the app and sign in. Use the same account on both devices.
  3. 3
    Get MPUs. In Account Management, choose Subscribe for Pro or a pack under Get MPUs. There is no free trial. Plans and pricing.
  4. 4
    Create a project. Start a new Hosted project. It stays private until you share it.
  5. 5
    Describe your first change. Type what you want in the message bar, or attach a photo or sketch. If Sawyer asks a question, reply in the conversation so the request can continue.
  6. 6
    Review it in 3D. Orbit, zoom and measure the model, then ask for the next change. Every change is a new version, and you can go back to an earlier one from Versions.
  7. 7
    Take the files with you. Open Documents for drawings and STEP, IFC or GLB files to use in other tools.
  8. 8
    Get help. The Support page explains how to report a problem.

Generated models and files need independent review for what you intend to build. The checks do not certify structural safety, building-code compliance, materials purchasing or permit acceptance. Early outside-user tests will focus on simple solid-volume jobs such as a slab or footing.

Bring Your Own Infra: run your own setup

With Bring Your Own Infra, your projects live in your own GitHub account and use your own AI provider, and the app helps you connect both. It is a complete path for people who want that control. You pay your AI provider directly for every request.

New to GitHub?

Bring Your Own Infra keeps your project in your own free GitHub account. If you have never used GitHub, this step-by-step guide shows how to sign up, protect your account, and connect MakeItOurs, what the MakeItOurs GitHub App can and can't access, and how to remove it. Rather not use GitHub at all? Hosted needs no GitHub account.

Need an AI key?

Bring Your Own Infra also uses your own AI account. This guide shows how to get an API key from OpenRouter, set a spending limit, paste it into MakeItOurs, and replace it later. Hosted includes the AI, so it needs no key.
