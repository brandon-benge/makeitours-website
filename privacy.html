Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026 · Benge Software LLC

MakeItOurs ("the app") is published by Benge Software LLC ("we," "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and, importantly, that we do not operate a backend server that receives, stores, or processes your projects or personal data. On Mac, MakeItOurs is local-first: your project source lives on your device, and GitHub is optional. On iPhone and iPad, MakeItOurs is GitHub-first: the app keeps no local copy of your project — it works entirely through a GitHub repository, either your own or one shared with you. For the technical detail behind how credentials are transported and stored, see Security.

What stays on your device

On Mac, your project files, app settings, and your GitHub authorization are stored only in your device's local storage and Keychain — we never receive, transmit to our own servers, or store a copy of them. On iPhone and iPad there is no local copy of your project, but your GitHub authorization is stored the same way, in Keychain.

Your AI provider API key

On Mac, if you run OpenCode locally, your API key is stored only in Keychain and used to talk directly to your AI provider — we never see it, and it never leaves your device.

On iPhone and iPad, AI requests run through your repository's GitHub Actions rather than on the phone itself. Your API key is encrypted on your device — using your repository's own public key — and uploaded to GitHub as an encrypted repository secret, so GitHub does store it, as ciphertext only your repository's own Actions runs can decrypt. We still never receive it: encryption happens on your device before anything is sent, and only that ciphertext travels, directly to GitHub, never to us.

What you choose to connect

GitHub

On Mac, connecting GitHub is optional — you can create, build, and save projects entirely locally. On iPhone and iPad, GitHub is required, since the app keeps no local copy of your project. Either way, once authorized, the app talks directly to GitHub's API using your authorization. Your project source, revision history, and any published preview site are stored in that GitHub repository — yours, or one shared with you — governed by GitHub's own privacy policy.

Your AI provider

MakeItOurs does not run its own AI service. When you ask MakeItOurs to generate or change a design, the relevant project content and your request are sent to the AI provider you've configured with your own API key — for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter, Groq, xAI, DeepInfra, Mistral, or OpenCode. On Mac this happens directly from your device; on iPhone and iPad it happens from your repository's GitHub Actions run, using the encrypted key described above. That exchange is governed by the privacy policy of the provider you chose, not by us. We recommend reviewing your chosen provider's policy before connecting it.

iCloud sync

MakeItOurs automatically syncs your project catalog across your own devices using Apple's CloudKit — it isn't a setting you turn on separately, and it requires your device to be signed into iCloud. That sync is governed by Apple's privacy policy. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account, not on servers we operate.

What we (Benge Software LLC) collect

Because MakeItOurs has no first-party backend, we do not collect, receive, or store your project content, credentials, or usage data on our own infrastructure. Standard, aggregated App Store analytics provided by Apple (such as install counts and crash reports, if you opt in) may be available to us through App Store Connect, subject to Apple's privacy policy. We do not sell personal data, because we do not hold any to sell.

Children's privacy

MakeItOurs is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
